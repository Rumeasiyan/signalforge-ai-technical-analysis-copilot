# SignalForge Demo Script (Beginner-Friendly)

Use this exact script for audiences with no AI or finance background.

Goal of the demo:

- show how AI + finance signals are merged into one practical decision workflow
- show how the system reduces manual effort and confusion
- show how users move from raw data to explainable decisions faster

---

## Demo setup (before you speak)

1. Sign in and open `/dashboard`.
2. Make sure `AAPL` already has at least one analysis in `/analysis`.
3. Keep the floating AI chat button visible.

---

## Opening script (say this first, 30-45 sec)

"Most people looking at markets face two problems: too much data and too little clarity. SignalForge combines market data and AI explanations so users can make faster, more consistent, and more explainable decisions. Today I will show how each part of the system works in plain language."

---

## Part 1: Dashboard (`/dashboard`) - 2 minutes

Say:

"This is the command center. Instead of opening many tools, users start here and instantly see what needs attention."

Show each widget and say exactly:

1. **Watchlist**
   - "This shows how many instruments we are actively tracking."
2. **Recent analyses**
   - "This tells us how much market review has already been completed."
3. **Strong bullish / bearish / neutral sections**
   - "These categorize opportunities by direction and clarity, so users can prioritize quickly."
4. **Instruments needing attention**
   - "These are symbols where signals are weak, mixed, or risky."
5. **Quick analysis form**
   - "Here we can run a fresh AI-assisted analysis in seconds."

Transition line:

"Now I will open one symbol and show how AI and market data combine into a full explanation."

---

## Part 2: Analysis Desk (`/analysis`) - 6 minutes

Action steps:

1. Search `AAPL`.
2. Choose `Medium-term`.
3. Click `Run AI analysis`.

Then explain each widget in this order:

### 2.1 Analysis Header

Say:

"This header confirms what we are analyzing: the instrument, market type, timeframe, and watchlist status."

### 2.2 Signal Scores (Directional Bias, Setup Quality, Confidence, Snapshot)

Say:

- "Directional Bias is the current direction estimate: bullish, bearish, or neutral."
- "Setup Quality measures how clean the market structure looks."
- "Confidence measures how reliable the current read is right now."
- "Snapshot tells us when this read was generated."

### 2.3 Market Commentary

Say:

"This is where AI translates technical signals into plain English. Instead of raw numbers, users get a readable explanation of what the market is doing."

### 2.4 Key Levels (Support and Resistance)

Say:

- "Support zones are areas where price may hold."
- "Resistance zones are areas where price may struggle."
- "These levels help users plan entries, exits, and invalidation."

### 2.5 Price Chart (Data used in analysis)

Say:

"This chart shows the exact price data behind the analysis. So users can see the evidence, not just a conclusion."

### 2.6 Data Exports and Traceability

Click CSV buttons and say:

- "Candles CSV exports raw market bars."
- "Analysis CSV exports the latest analysis snapshot."
- "This is useful for audits, compliance, reporting, and deeper offline review."

### 2.7 AI Explanation Behind Prediction

Say:

"This section explains why the system gave this direction and score, using trend, momentum, participation, and volatility."

### 2.8 Indicator Interpretation

Say:

"Here we combine indicators together. We do not treat indicators in isolation. This avoids common mistakes from looking at one signal alone."

### 2.9 Multi-Timeframe View

Say:

"This compares short-term, medium-term, and long-term views. If they align, conviction is higher. If they conflict, risk is higher."

### 2.10 Scenario Planning and Risk Read

Say:

- "Bullish scenario shows what must happen for upside continuation."
- "Bearish scenario shows downside risk conditions."
- "Neutral scenario shows when to wait instead of forcing a trade."
- "Risk read explains breakout failure and reversal risk."

### 2.11 Floating AI Chat (bottom-right)

Click a widget first (for focus), open chat, ask:

`Explain this widget like I am new to trading.`

Then say:

"This assistant is context-aware. It knows the selected widget and current analysis context. It explains in beginner language and asks follow-up questions to guide the user."

---

## Part 3: Watchlist (`/watchlist`) - 2 minutes

Say:

"This is the daily monitoring workspace. Users compare multiple instruments quickly and decide where to focus."

Explain widgets:

1. instrument identity card
2. latest bias and scores
3. summary commentary
4. refresh analysis action
5. open full analysis action

Key line:

"This turns watchlist review from a manual routine into a structured process."

---

## Part 4: History (`/history`) - 1.5 minutes

Say:

"This is the learning and accountability layer. Users can revisit past analyses and see if market bias changed over time."

Explain widgets:

1. historical analysis cards
2. bias change marker
3. setup/confidence over time
4. reopen analysis link

Key line:

"This helps teams review decision quality, not just outcomes."

---

## AI + Finance value statement (say this near the end)

"SignalForge combines quantitative finance signals with AI explanation. Finance gives the evidence. AI gives the clarity. Together they reduce analysis fatigue, improve consistency, and help users make more efficient decisions."

---

## Closing script (final 20 sec)

"In short, SignalForge is not a black-box predictor. It is an explainable decision-support system that helps people move from market noise to structured action quickly and responsibly."
